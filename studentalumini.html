Sanjay (CS '18) - SDE at Amazon May 15, 4:12 PM
Hi Rahul! Great question. I'd recommend starting with "Cracking the Coding Interview" as your bible. For FAANG, you need to be very comfortable with medium-level Leetcode problems (can solve in 20-25 mins). Focus on arrays, strings, trees, graphs, and DP.
Neha (CS '20) - SWE at Google May 15, 4:30 PM
Adding to what Sanjay said, system design becomes important for higher levels. For new grads, focus on coding first but do read about basic system design concepts. Also, practice explaining your thought process out loud - communication is key!
